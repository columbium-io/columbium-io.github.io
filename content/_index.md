### Active Research Tracks

<div class="research-track">
<strong>Intelligent Family Management</strong><br>
AI-driven systems for household time and financial planning. We build predictive models that learn family patterns, optimize schedules against competing priorities, and surface actionable financial insights—turning raw household data into a coherent operating picture.
</div>

<div class="research-track">
<strong>Agentic Coaching for Sports Performance</strong><br>
Autonomous coaching agents that adapt to individual athletes in real time. By integrating biomechanical priors, historical performance data, and sequential decision frameworks, we deliver personalized training programs that evolve with each session.
</div>
